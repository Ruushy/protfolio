About

Building and breaking web applications, on purpose.

I'm an offensive-security-focused full-stack developer and web penetration tester, with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and hands-on experience across web application penetration testing, bug bounty hunting, vulnerability research, and security automation.

My background sits at the intersection of building and breaking: strong software engineering skills in the MERN stack and Django, paired with a practical, attacker's understanding of how web applications fail. That combination lets me design systems that hold up under scrutiny, and find the weaknesses in systems that don't.

Most of my time goes into building custom offensive-security tooling — reconnaissance frameworks, vulnerability scanners, and automated reporting pipelines — to streamline the full penetration testing and bug bounty workflow. I've shipped a bug bounty automation framework, a JavaScript secret-discovery tool, a full security scanning platform, and more than a dozen interactive cybersecurity training labs, several of which are the first of their kind written in the Somali language.

I work independently in Linux environments, with solid networking fundamentals, and I'm currently pursuing a Master's degree in Cybersecurity while continuing to build in public.
